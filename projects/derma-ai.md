# Derma AI

- **One-line summary:** A machine learning framework that diagnoses skin conditions across diverse skin tones and textures, built to address bias in dermatological AI datasets.
- **When:** August 2024
- **Where / program:** Microsoft's Annual BAM AI Hackathon, during my Microsoft Software Engineering Internship (placed 2nd of 8 teams)
- **My role:** Team lead for a 4-person team, dividing the work across model, data, and UI
- **Tools:** Python, Streamlit, Flask

## The problem

Many dermatology AI datasets are dominated by lighter skin tones, so models trained on them can perform worse for people with darker skin or different skin textures. We set out to build a diagnostic framework that works fairly across a wider range of skin tones and textures.

## What I did

1. Led the team through a time-constrained hackathon build and split the work into three tracks: model, data, and UI.
2. Worked on the data side to account for diversity in skin tones and textures and reduce dataset bias.
3. Built and trained an ML model to classify skin conditions from images.
4. Wrapped the model in a web app using Flask for the backend and Streamlit for the interface.
5. Tested the full flow end to end and presented the finished project to the judges.
